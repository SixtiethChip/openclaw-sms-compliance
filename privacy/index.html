Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how StevenG handles information in connection with the OpenClaw SMS Assistant.

Information the service may process

The OpenClaw SMS Assistant may process your mobile phone number, SMS or MMS message contents, information you voluntarily provide through messages, and operational information needed to provide requested assistant functions.

How information is used

Information may be used to provide conversational assistant responses, reminders, scheduling information, task updates, requested notifications, and responses to your requests, and to operate and secure the messaging service.

Sharing and service providers

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data, mobile information, or personal information with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

Service providers necessary to operate the service, such as telecommunications, hosting, AI, and technology providers, may process information solely as necessary to provide the requested service and subject to applicable privacy and security practices.
